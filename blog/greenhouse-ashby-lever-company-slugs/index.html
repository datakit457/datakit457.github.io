Verified company slugs for Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever job APIs (70 tech companies)

Disclosure: I'm the author of the Actor mentioned at the end (datakit/ats-jobs-feed). The list itself is free to use.

Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever all expose public job-board JSON, but you need the company's board slug, and it is often not what you would guess. I probed about 90 well-known tech company names against the three APIs on 2026-11-09 and kept the ones that returned open jobs. Counts are a snapshot and change daily. Each company is listed under the system where it has the most jobs; a few (Klaviyo, Ramp) have stale boards on a second system.

Endpoints used:

  • Greenhouse: https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/<slug>/jobs
  • Lever: https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/<slug>?mode=json
  • Ashby: https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/<slug>

Names that returned nothing

About 20 of the names I tried (including Canva, Rippling, Deel, Retool and Mistral) returned nothing under the obvious slug on all three APIs. I did not check why: they may use another ATS, a custom careers page, or a different slug. Check the careers page URL before guessing.

Using the list

Paste any lines above into the companies input of ATS Jobs Feed, set mode to new-jobs-only and keywords like engineer, and run it daily to get only newly posted roles. Or call the three endpoints yourself with requests; the slug is the only input.
