List UK food businesses with a low hygiene rating (FHRS API, Python)

Disclosure: I'm the author of the Actor mentioned at the end (datakit/uk-new-food-business-leads). The API in the examples is the Food Standards Agency's open API and you can use it directly.

Pest-control, deep-cleaning and compliance-consultancy firms, and local journalists, all want the same list: food businesses in an area whose hygiene rating is 2 or lower. The FSA publishes it as open data (Open Government Licence v3), no key needed.

Query it directly

Rating filters work on the /Establishments endpoint. ratingKey=2 with ratingOperatorKey=LessThanOrEqual returns ratings 0, 1 and 2.

import requests
H = {"x-api-version": "2", "accept": "application/json"}
r = requests.get("https://api.ratings.food.gov.uk/Establishments", headers=H, params={
    "localAuthorityId": 401,            # Middlesbrough; list IDs via /Authorities/basic
    "ratingKey": 2, "ratingOperatorKey": "LessThanOrEqual",
    "pageSize": 100, "pageNumber": 1}).json()
print(r["meta"]["totalCount"], "businesses rated 2 or lower")
for e in r["establishments"]:
    print(e["BusinessName"], e["PostCode"], e["RatingValue"], e["RatingDate"][:10], e["BusinessType"])

I ran this on 2026-11-08 for Middlesbrough: 35 businesses rated 2 or lower. Each row has name, address, postcode, business type, rating, inspection date and coordinates.

Caveats

  • Ratings are a snapshot of the last inspection; a business may have improved and not yet been re-inspected. Check RatingDate.
  • The data has no contact details. Treat it as a prospect or story lead, and contact businesses in line with PECR/GDPR rules.
  • To cover a region, loop over its local authority IDs and be polite with request rate.

Want new businesses instead?

The same register also shows businesses that have just registered and await their first inspection. datakit/uk-new-food-business-leads keeps state between runs and returns only businesses not seen before, filtered by region or postcode, so you can schedule a weekly list.
