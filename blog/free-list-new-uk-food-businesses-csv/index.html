Free list of new UK food businesses awaiting their first hygiene inspection (CSV, by region)

Disclosure: I built the generator behind these lists (and the Apify Actor datakit/uk-new-food-business-leads). The lists are free and the underlying data is the Food Standards Agency's open API, under the Open Government Licence v3.

When a café, takeaway or caterer registers with its local council, it appears on the FSA's food hygiene register marked Awaiting Inspection until an officer visits. That makes the "awaiting inspection" list a decent proxy for newly opened food businesses: the people who suddenly need a POS system, packaging, insurance, an accountant, a web page or a food-safety course.

I publish that list for free, split by region, as CSV and RSS: UK new food businesses.

What is in it (snapshot 2026-11-14)

Each row has business name, type, address, postcode, council, region, the date I first saw it, and a link to the official FSA record.

How to use it for prospecting

1. Download your region's CSV, or all-uk.csv. 2. Filter business_type (for example "Restaurant/Cafe/Canteen" or "Takeaway/sandwich shop") and council to your patch. 3. Sort by first_listed. The first run is marked baseline; from the next weekly run, rows carry the date they first appeared, so the "new this week" rows are the freshest. 4. Look each business up before contacting it. Some trading names are a sole trader's own name; handle those under UK GDPR and PECR rules (B2B marketing to sole traders is treated like consumer marketing for email).

Quick filter in Python:

import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_csv("https://datakit457.github.io/uk-new-food-businesses/london.csv")
cafes = df[df.business_type.str.contains("Restaurant", na=False)]
print(cafes[["business_name", "postcode", "first_listed"]].head(20))

Limits

  • "Awaiting inspection" also includes businesses that have operated for a while but not been visited yet, so it is a proxy for "new", not proof.
  • Councils differ in how quickly they inspect, so counts per region say more about inspection backlog than about openings.
  • It carries no phone or email; the FSA does not publish them.
