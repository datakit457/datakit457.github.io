Weekly list of new UK food businesses (FSA hygiene ratings data)

An Apify Actor that reads the official Food Standards Agency (FSA) register and outputs only the food businesses that are new since your last run. Free to try on the Apify free plan.

Who it is for

  • POS/EPOS, payments and booking software sellers
  • Food wholesalers, packaging, cleaning and pest-control suppliers
  • Insurance brokers, accountants and business-bank onboarding teams

How it works

  1. First run stores a baseline for your stateKey.
  2. Each later run outputs only new businesses, labelled awaiting_first_inspection (just registered), recently_rated or new_on_register.
  3. Filter by council name or postcode prefix; export to CSV, JSON, Google Sheets, Slack or a webhook.

Example row

{"BusinessName": "Airport Cafe", "BusinessType": "Restaurant/Cafe/Canteen", "leadSignal": "awaiting_first_inspection"}

Data and limits

Source: FSA open API, Open Government Licence v3. No contact details are included, and registration appears after the council processes it, so expect a lag of days to weeks.

Prefer to do it yourself? See the 15-line Python tutorial.

FAQ

Where does the data come from?

The Food Standards Agency's open FHRS API (Open Government Licence v3).

How soon does a new business show up?

Only after its local council registers it and the FSA publishes it, so expect days to weeks.

Does it include phone numbers or emails?

No. It outputs business name, type, address and rating fields only.

How much does it cost?

The Actor runs on the Apify free plan; you pay Apify's normal usage costs on larger runs.
