Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Recruitee jobs in one feed, new postings only

An Apify Actor that reads the official public job-board APIs of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Recruitee for the companies you list, normalizes every job to one format, and in newSinceLastRun mode returns only jobs it has not emitted before.

Who it is for

  • Niche job boards and newsletters ("AI startups", "climate tech") built from a fixed list of employers
  • Recruiters and sales teams watching hiring as a buying signal at target accounts
  • Job seekers tracking dozens of companies without visiting each careers page

Input

A companies list (greenhouse:stripe, lever:palantir, ashby:openai, workable:huggingface, smartrecruiters:smartrecruiters, recruitee:bunq, or a careers-page URL), optional keyword, location and remote-only filters, and a stateKey naming your feed's memory.

Example row

{"title": "Technical Program Manager, Compute Infrastructure", "company": "openai", "location": "San Francisco", "remote": true, "salary": {"min": 207000, "max": 285000, "currency": "USD"}, "ats": "ashby"}

Data and limits

Uses each vendor's documented public job-board endpoint: no login, no browser, no personal data. Salary appears only where the employer publishes it. Schedule it daily and send results to Sheets, Slack or a webhook.
