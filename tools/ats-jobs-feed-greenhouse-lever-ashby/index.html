Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor and Workday jobs in one feed, new postings only

An Apify Actor that reads the public job-board APIs of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor and Workday for the companies you list, normalizes every job to one format, and in newSinceLastRun mode returns only jobs it has not emitted before.

Who it is for

  • Niche job boards and newsletters ("AI startups", "climate tech") built from a fixed list of employers
  • Recruiters and sales teams watching hiring as a buying signal at target accounts
  • Job seekers tracking dozens of companies without visiting each careers page

Input

A companies list (greenhouse:stripe, lever:palantir, ashby:openai, workable:huggingface, smartrecruiters:smartrecruiters, recruitee:bunq, personio:mobimeo, or a careers-page URL; Workday needs the full URL such as https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite), optional keyword, location and remote-only filters, and a stateKey naming your feed's memory.

No company list? Search the startup directory

Set directory to startups and it scans the job boards of 650+ tech companies (Y Combinator alumni and well-known tech firms on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable, verified November 2026). Add keywords and locations, e.g. every new data engineer job in London, with the real company name. Filter by sector with directoryIndustries (Fintech, Healthcare, B2B...).

Example row

{"title": "Technical Program Manager, Compute Infrastructure", "company": "openai", "location": "San Francisco", "remote": true, "salary": {"min": 207000, "max": 285000, "currency": "USD"}, "ats": "ashby"}

Data and limits

Uses each vendor's documented public job-board endpoint: no login, no browser, no personal data. Salary appears only where the employer publishes it. Schedule it daily and send results to Sheets, Slack or a webhook.

FAQ

Which job platforms are supported?

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor and Workday (paste the myworkdayjobs.com careers URL).

How do I specify a company?

Use prefix:slug such as greenhouse:stripe, or paste the company's careers-page URL.

Is salary included?

Only when the employer publishes it in the job-board data.

Does it use a browser or login?

No, it calls each vendor's public job-board endpoint.
