Kalibrr jobs scraper: new Philippine job listings since your last run

An Apify Actor that collects public Kalibrr job listings and, in newSinceLastRun mode, returns only jobs it has not shown you before.

Who it is for

  • Builders of job boards, newsletters and Telegram/Slack job-alert channels
  • Recruiters and staffing agencies tracking who is hiring in Manila, Cebu and elsewhere
  • Researchers measuring demand for roles and skills

Input

Keywords, cities, job function, work-from-home only, include description, max items, and a stateKey naming your feed's memory.

Example row

{"title": "Data Analyst | Emerging Technologies", "company": "Questronix Corporation", "city": "Makati", "hybrid": true}

Data and limits

Public job-search pages only, no login, robots.txt permitting; no applicant data. Schedule it daily and connect Google Sheets or a webhook.

FAQ

Does it need a Kalibrr login?

No. It reads public job-search results only.

How do I get only new jobs?

Use newSinceLastRun mode with a fixed stateKey; the Actor remembers which job IDs it already returned.

Can I filter by city or work-from-home?

Yes: set cities and the work-from-home option in the input.

Is it affiliated with Kalibrr?

No, it is an independent tool.
