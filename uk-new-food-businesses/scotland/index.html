About this data

Source: Food Standards Agency food hygiene rating data (FHRS, and FHIS in Scotland), reused under the Open Government Licence v3. A business shows as awaiting inspection once its council has registered it and before its first hygiene inspection, so most entries are new openings or new owners. Councils ask new food businesses to register at least 28 days before they open, so many of these are not trading yet.
