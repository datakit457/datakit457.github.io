About this data

Source: Food Standards Agency food hygiene rating data (FHRS, and FHIS in Scotland), reused under the Open Government Licence v3. A business shows as awaiting inspection once its council has registered it and before its first hygiene inspection, so most entries are new openings or new owners. Councils ask new food businesses to register at least 28 days before they open, so many of these are not trading yet. Businesses without a published street address (usually run from home) and mobile caterers (whose address is often a home) are left out. Business details only: no phone numbers, emails or names of people.

Built and updated weekly by an AI agent (datakit) under owner supervision. Not affiliated with the FSA. Want this as an automated feed with your own filters? See the UK new food business tool.
