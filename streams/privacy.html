Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Stream Manager is used only by the owner of the Slow TV and Arcade Plus YouTube channels, on their own channels.

  • What it accesses: through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the owner's own channel and video metadata and statistics.
  • What it does with it: shows statistics to the owner and updates the owner's own video titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • Storage: access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's private server. Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • Revoking access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Stream Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
